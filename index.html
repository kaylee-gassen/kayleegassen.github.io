PROJECT 02
Crisis Program Launch & COVID-19 Services Manual
Friends House Retirement Community · Crisis Operations & Remote Delivery
Summer 2020
The Problem
Facility locked down overnight with no remote programming in place.

COVID hit and the facility locked down overnight. Twenty residents, many with limited mobility or cognitive impairment, suddenly had no programming, no family visits, and nothing to structure their days, and we came to provide assistance.

Solution
Built a virtual delivery model and documented it for future use.

I built a remote delivery model using Teams and Google Workspace, adapted sessions for different ability levels, and co-wrote a COVID Recreation Manual with a three-person team so the facility had a manual and curriculum going forward.

My Story

A bit of background.

I care about people, and the way I've always shown that is by fixing the things standing in their way. Before any of this, I was a full time student athlete. That's where I learned to manage a packed schedule, work well on my own, and pull my weight on a team that was counting on me.

At Mountain Springs Recovery I built the recreational therapy program from nothing. Hired the team, designed the curriculum, fixed a broken intake process. Client satisfaction went from 47% to 86% in eight months. I moved to Denver Children's Home on purpose. I wanted to help build a youth program and get hands on experience in a different part of healthcare. It gave me something Mountain Springs didn't. Working directly with youth in a high needs environment. Managing independent living programming. Keeping communication moving between clinical teams, families, and outside agencies.

Healthcare doesn't leave room for error. A missed compliance deadline. A gap in the schedule. A new hire who wasn't onboarded right. Five years in that world made me precise about documentation, clear about who owns what, and calm when things start to go sideways. I have learned in my time how to catch certain mistakes before they become huge problems, and that is something I love to do, is solve complex probelms.

Solutions Implemented

1
Power BI Operations Dashboard
Built a centralized Power BI dashboard consolidating scheduling efficiency, compliance rates, engagement trends, staff utilization, and admissions data. Reduced manual reporting from 3–4 hours to under 30 minutes weekly.
2
Standardized Meeting Cadence & Dependency Framework
Implemented structured weekly meetings with pre-defined agenda templates and 48-hour conflict resolution protocols. Reduced scheduling conflicts from 28% to 13%.
3
EMR Documentation Protocol
Created standardized checklists, real-time audit triggers, and weekly compliance reviews. Achieved 100% documentation compliance within 60 days of implementation.
4
Evidence-Based Curriculum — 3 Treatment Tracks
Standardized curricula reducing process variation and enabling consistent, measurable outcomes. Contributed to 15% admissions increase and 30-day retention improvement.

Cross-Department Communication Framework
Documented handoff protocols, RACI matrix, and stakeholder communication standards — eliminating ambiguity in role ownership and escalation paths.
